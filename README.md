# SQL Sales Analysis Project

## Objective
Sales performance, inventory status, revenue trends and basket analysis using SQL.

## Dataset
AdventureWorks2017.

## SQL Skills Used
- Aggregation: SUM, COUNT, GROUP BY,...
- Joins across multiple tables
- Window functions
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Date-based analysis (YEAR, MONTH, QUARTER)
- Business analysis

## Key Analysis
- Analyzed current inventory levels across product categories to provide visibility into stock status.
- Evaluated total revenue of products with transaction activity in 2014, broken down by product category.
- Assessed the performance of the Bikes category in 2014, including sales volume, total revenue, and percentage contribution to overall category sales.
- Conducted monthly sales analysis with a focused review of June 2014 performance.
- Analyzed historical sales trends of product categories across multiple years.
- Performed quarter-over-quarter (QoQ) revenue comparison between Q2 2014 and Q1 2014.
- Performed year-over-year (YoY) revenue comparison between Q2 2014 and Q2 of the previous year.
- Analyzed quarterly revenue trends by product category over time.
- Evaluated purchasing reasons by product category across historical periods.
- Identify products frequently purchased together to support cross-selling and bundle promotions.
